Reflection

Performance of the Agents & How They Compared to My Expectations

Overall, the agents did a good job. The boss called the agents I expected it to call for each ticket, but often the order of the calls differed from what I expected. For example, for ticket 102, I thought the boss would call facilities first to examine the lease terms and confirm them, but the boss ended up calling accounting first to verify that there was enough money to pay the rent.

At times, agents surfaced situations that I wasn't aware of when I wrote the expected results. For instance, for ticket 101, the product in question is out of stock, so instead of just inventory being called, inventory also made a call out to accounting.

What Could Have Been Simplified

I think some of the capabilities of accounting could have been merged into inventory. Right now, accounting has the ability to check the profit margins of products, but it could be easier for inventory (which has to read product SKUs anyway) to examine the margins of products. Similarly, since inventory reads the stock info and the vendor info, having inventory prepare purchase orders could be easier compared to making accounting do so.

Problems that can be solved by my agents

  1. Restock items. Even though my agent is unable to restock the item related to ticket 101, with enough cash and no blocking invoices, it should be able to restock items with no issue.
  2. Finding how low of a discount the boss can give for any product in the database.
  3. Creating a restocking plan based on the remaining cash.

Problems that can't be solved by my agents

  1. Anything outside of the box — for example, the agent can't order anything new or from any vendor outside the database.
  2. The agents don't give the human an option to approve a partial payment, so we can't pay an invoice partially.
  3. There is no way to record revenue / money coming in to the shop.
